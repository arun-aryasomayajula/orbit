# Orbit — product workspace guide

Orbit proposes; you decide.
Agents research, draft, and file work on their own, then stop at every judgment call and wait for you.
Nothing is merged, published, or committed to your product without your explicit decision.

## The five views

| View | Question it answers | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** | What changed? What needs me? | Scan the brief: decisions waiting, work in flight, artifacts that landed. |
| **Decisions** | What is blocked on my judgment? | Every pending gate in one place — answer, approve, revise, publish, or reject. |
| **Work** | What are we learning or making? | PRDs, insights, surveys, and watches. Ask a question or start a PRD here. |
| **Library** | What can I read, reuse, or share? | Published PRDs, insight and survey reports, reference knowledge — rendered as documents. |
| **Setup** | What is connected? | Secrets, data and publishing connections, watches, cadence. JSON stays optional. |

## Your day in two minutes

1. Open Today.
   If it says "Clear skies", nothing is blocked on you.
2. If decisions are waiting, open Decisions and work top-down.
   Each card carries its context, evidence, and a small set of labeled actions.
3. New questions go in through Work ("Ask a question" or "New PRD").
   Orbit drafts; the result comes back to Decisions or lands in Library.

## What the words mean

- **Watch** — a standing question Orbit re-answers on a cadence, filing a fresh insight each time.
- **Insight** — a grounded answer to one product question. Every number cites its source; Orbit abstains rather than guesses.
- **PRD run** — a staged pipeline (intake → research → synthesis → draft → review) with your approval gates between stages.
- **Publish** — pushing a signed-off PRD to Confluence, or filing an insight as a Jira issue. Always started by you.
- **Escalation** — the agent stopped mid-work to ask you something. Your answer joins the work contract.

## Engineering diagnostics

The rail's **Engineering** entry opens the queue mechanics, branches, agents, QA, and loop-health views.
They are diagnostics: useful when something looks stuck, never required for product work.
